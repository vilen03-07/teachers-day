A little something

for every teacher

Every journey begins
with a lesson...

And behind so many of our journeys,
there was a teacher.

Today's Lesson
Believe in yourself.

You taught us more
than what was written in a book.

To ask questions.

To try again when we failed.

To be curious.

To be brave.

To keep learning.

Sometimes, you may not realize
how much a few words from you
meant to us.

Things you may never hear

Sometimes,
the smallest moments
mean the most.

"Well done."

Those two words may have given someone the confidence to keep going.

"Try again."

They may have taught someone that failure isn't the end.

"I believe in you."

Those words may have helped someone believe in themselves.

"It's okay to make mistakes."

They may have made learning feel safe.

You may remember a lesson.

We may remember
how you made us feel.
